# TFGBV-specific text dataset development plan

**Status:** Research preparation only. No TFGBV dataset has been collected under this plan; no source is pre-approved.

The current JamiiForums-derived corpus is a general offensive-language resource, not TFGBV data. Its dataset card's license statement does not by itself establish rights to underlying posts or source-site terms. It must not be treated as a TFGBV evaluation corpus or as evidence of TFGBV model capability.

The authoritative detailed plan is now the linked protocol package:

- [Research protocol](tfgbv_research_protocol.md) — objectives, scope, approval gates, source strategy, sampling, annotation, evaluation and prohibited activities.
- [Annotation handbook](annotation_guidelines_draft.md) — proposed multilabel taxonomy, uncertainty, language review and annotator safeguards.
- [Schema and data dictionary](dataset_schema_and_data_dictionary.md) — minimized fields, privacy controls, versioning and per-source approval register template.
- [Approval and collection checklist](data_collection_and_ethics_checklist.md) — evidence, status and accountable-role placeholders.
- [Controlled pilot plan](pilot_study_plan.md) — planning-only pilot size, annotation overlap, metrics, review and go/no-go conditions.
- [Risk register](dataset_risk_register.md) — qualitative risks, safeguards and review ownership.
- [Model research roadmap](model_research_roadmap.md) — future model study stages after separate approval.

Do not scrape websites, forums, social media, or messaging platforms; download actual victim content; collect private messages, leaked material, intimate imagery, or identifying details; annotate; train; or deploy until all applicable institutional, ethics, legal, privacy, source, and safety approvals are documented. Public availability is not blanket permission. AI research remains separate from SOS, evidence, audio, BLE, notifications, and emergency response.
